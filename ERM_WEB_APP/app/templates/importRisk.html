Enterprise Rollover Rules & Data Integrity
  • Unique Fiscal Years: The destination financial year must differ from the source year, and cannot already contain risk records.
  • Automated Numbering: Each risk is re-indexed with department-specific sequence numbers matching the target FY.
  • Fresh Cycle Initialization: Risk descriptions and treatments are replicated, while action progress status and followup dates are reset for the new review cycle.
  • Transactional Integrity: If any unexpected error occurs during migration, all modifications are rolled back automatically with zero data corruption.
