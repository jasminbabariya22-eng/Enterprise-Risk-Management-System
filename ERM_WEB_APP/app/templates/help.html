5×5 Enterprise Risk Matrix

Standardized Impact & Likelihood Assessment Framework
Core Standard

In the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) system, every risk is evaluated on a calibrated 5×5 Matrix where Risk Severity Score = Likelihood (L) × Impact (I). Both inherent (pre-control) and current (post-control) scores are computed using these unified corporate thresholds:

1 – 4
Low Severity
Manage by routine controls
5 – 9
Medium Severity
Functional review required
10 – 16
High Severity

20 – 25

Risk Registration & Bulk Import

Registering individual risks and importing bulk inventories
Open Register →
Single Risk Registration
  1. Navigate to Risk Register and click Create Risk.
  2. Enter the primary Risk Name, Description, and select your Functional Department.
  3. Assign the primary Risk Owner (and optional Co-Owner) responsible for governance.
  4. Score both Inherent Risk (initial severity) and Current Risk (residual severity after existing controls).
  5. Submit the risk. It will enter the 3-Stage Approval Pipeline starting at Stage 1.
Bulk Excel Import
  1. Open the Import Risk or Upload Data page.
  2. Download the authorized ERM Excel template containing validated column headers.
  3. Populate rows with mandatory fields: Department, Risk Name, Inherent Likelihood, Inherent Impact.
  4. Upload the .xlsx file. The parser runs validation checks on row integrity and scoring ranges.
  5. Review the preview table, confirm verification, and commit to the enterprise database.
Validation Rule: Likelihood and Impact scores must be integers between 1 and 5. Unknown department names in uploaded spreadsheets will trigger row validation errors.

3-Stage Governance Approval Workflow

Rigorous three-tier review and sign-off procedure
Sequential Process

All newly created or amended enterprise risks undergo a strict 3-tier sequential review before activation:

Stage 1 Department Scope
Functional Head Review

Departmental head verifies operational accuracy, ownership assignment, and control feasibility. Can Approve to advance or Return for Revision.

Stage 2 Enterprise Scope
Risk Manager Verification

Central Risk Management verifies cross-functional consistency, matrix calibration, and mitigation alignment across departments.

Stage 3 Executive Sign-Off
Risk Head Final Approval

Executive Risk Head conducts final governance review and signs off. Once signed off, the risk becomes Approved & Active in executive dashboards.

What happens if a risk is returned/rejected?

If an approver returns a risk at any stage with remarks, its status transitions to Needs Revision / Returned. It immediately appears in the primary Risk Owner's Action Center queue with the action label Update & Resubmit. After updating details on Risk Details, resubmission restarts the review pipeline.

Risk Treatment & Action Plans

Formulating mitigation measures and tracking completion milestones
Mitigation Execution

For Medium, High, and Critical risks, risk owners establish actionable mitigation measures called Risk Treatment Action Plans to reduce residual impact:

1. Task Definition

Specify the exact action plan description, deliverables, required resources, and designated Action Owner.

2. Target Milestone

Establish a hard target completion date. Approaching and overdue target dates trigger high-priority alerts in the Action Center.

3. Progress Updates

Action Owners update status on the Action Plan portal (Assigned, In Progress, or Completed) with evidentiary notes.

Once an action plan is marked complete, the residual risk score should be reassessed to verify reduction in current likelihood or impact.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Resolutions for common questions and validation errors
FAQ Library

Inherent Risk represents the natural level of risk existing in the absence of any controls or mitigations. Current Risk (Residual Risk) is the actual remaining exposure assessed after taking into consideration all existing controls and risk treatment measures currently in place.

A 401 Unauthorized error occurs when your session token expires after a period of inactivity, or if you opened a direct URL bookmark without signing in. To recover, simply click the Sign In button on the 401 page to re-authenticate.

A 403 Access Denied error indicates that your user credentials are valid, but your current assigned role lacks authorization for the specific feature (for instance, only Admin, Risk Manager, and Risk Head roles may access the Executive Dashboard). If you need access, contact your System Administrator to evaluate your role scope.

The red bell badge in the top navigation represents your Unread Action Items. Items remain in your queue until the action is completed in the workflow (e.g. approved or updated), but viewing an item or clicking the checkmark toggles it as Read, decrementing the badge count.
